Smashed Avo Property Price Index

Pollywaffle data viz engine

Pollywaffle visualises Australian federal politicians' 2025 property declarations through the foolproof logic that smashed avo on toast is what's standing between young (and not so) Australians and home ownership.

This visualisation has mapped out how many years of breakfasts you would have to give up to afford the same number of properties as various Politicians - based on a median house price of $848,858 AUD and eating $22 avo on toast EVERY.SINGLE.DAY.
